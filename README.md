
Sonoya

Sonoya is a mobile management app (React Native / Expo) built out of a real-world need: while working in my parents' shop, I saw recurring management gaps and a lack of traceability around stock and revenue. Sonoya addresses that by bringing several income streams — resale of goods, milling machine services, firewood sales — together in one tool.

Every stock purchase is tracked as a distinct batch (lot), allowing the app to compute a real profit margin on each sale instead of a rough average. Bulk products (sold by the bag) can be broken down into retail kg on demand, with full traceability back to the original batch. The app also tracks expenses — shared or machine-specific — and gives a clear daily view of the shop's actual net result.
